# Evolution Orchestration

Use this skill when managing an iterative evolution goal.

- Establish the goal, non-goals, metric directions, absolute guardrails, budget, and approval checkpoints before dispatch.
- Record every baseline and candidate as a revision with a frozen content identity. Never use a mutable path alone when its bytes can change.
- Give proposal, implementation, and evaluation subagents bounded tasks with explicit parents, inputs, outputs, and evidence requirements.
- Keep parallel candidates as separate branches. Create a merge revision only when its complete parent set is explicit.
- Require evaluatable artifacts to bind every metric observation to an exact revision.
- Score comparable revisions on the same metric contract; expose missing observations and regressions instead of filling them with assumptions.
- Track resource and monetary cost with units, provenance, and confidence before selecting the next task.
- The governor may recommend a revision, but trusted policy and configured human checkpoints retain final promotion authority.
